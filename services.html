Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our landscaping services

How do eco-friendly landscaping practices differ from traditional methods?

Eco-friendly landscaping focuses on sustainability through water conservation, use of native plants, organic fertilizers, and creating habitats for local wildlife. Unlike traditional methods, we minimize chemical use, reduce water consumption, and work with nature rather than against it.

Do you use organic and chemical-free products?

Yes, we prioritize organic, natural, and chemical-free products for all our services. We use compost and organic fertilizers, employ natural pest control methods, and avoid synthetic chemicals that can harm the environment.

How can sustainable landscaping save me money in the long run?

Sustainable landscaping reduces water bills through efficient irrigation and drought-tolerant plants, lowers maintenance costs with appropriate plant selection, decreases fertilizer and pesticide expenses through organic approaches, and increases property value with a well-designed outdoor space.

What areas in the Western Cape do you serve?

We serve clients throughout the Western Cape, including Cape Town, Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, Paarl, and surrounding areas. Contact us to confirm availability in your specific location.
