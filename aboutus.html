About GreenRoots Landscaping Co.

Learn about our journey, values, and the team behind our eco-friendly landscaping services

Our Story

2015

GreenRoots was founded in Cape Town as a small family-run landscaping business with a focus on sustainable practices.

2017

We expanded our services to include eco-friendly garden design and began working with commercial clients.

2019

GreenRoots won the Western Cape Sustainable Business Award and expanded our team to include specialized horticulturists.

2022

We launched our community outreach program, helping schools and public spaces create sustainable gardens.

Present

Today, we serve clients across the Western Cape with a team of 15 dedicated landscaping professionals.

Our Mission

To create sustainable, beautiful outdoor spaces that enrich lives and respect the environment.

Our Vision

To be South Africa's leading eco-conscious landscaping brand, inspiring sustainable practices industry-wide.

Our Values & Approach

Sustainability Focus

We prioritize environmentally friendly materials, water-wise designs, and native plant species in all our projects.

Community Commitment

We believe in giving back through community projects, educational programs, and supporting local suppliers.

Eco-Friendly Practices

From composting green waste to using electric equipment, we minimize our environmental impact at every step.
